# Business problem

This portfolio examines whether customer purchasing patterns and experience can inform retention decisions in a historical marketplace dataset. It does not represent a commissioned engagement or measured company performance.

| Concept | Meaning in this project |
| --- | --- |
| Acquisition | A customer's first observed purchase; not necessarily their first-ever purchase |
| Retention | Continued purchasing over an explicitly defined follow-up period |
| Repeat purchase | Another observed order; closely spaced orders may reflect one shopping episode |
| Churn | A future operational inactivity definition; not yet implemented |
| CLV | Expected future customer value over a stated horizon; not yet estimated |
| Profitability | Revenue less relevant costs; full cost data is unavailable |

The next question is whether first-order review score is associated with behavioral repeat purchasing. If a relationship appears, it could motivate further customer-experience investigation. It would not prove that improving reviews causes retention.

Future segment comparisons could inform hypotheses for retention campaigns. Budget allocation requires acquisition costs, margins and intervention evidence that this repository does not currently supply.
