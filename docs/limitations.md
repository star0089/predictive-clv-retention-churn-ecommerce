# Limitations

- The reported figures cannot be reproduced from the repository today.
- Olist is historical marketplace data; findings may not generalize to current stores or different business models.
- First observed purchase is not necessarily first-ever purchase. Persistent identifiers may not perfectly represent people or households.
- The analysis window and seven-day threshold are provisional. No validated churn label exists.
- Customers near the end of the dataset have less follow-up time. Treating them all as non-repeat customers biases comparisons.
- Missing and multiple reviews can distort group comparisons; review timing can create leakage.
- Timestamp parsing depends on the actual file representation. The earlier reported day-month-year format must not be assumed universally.
- Repeat behavior and review scores may share confounders. Association does not establish causality.
- Payment value and transaction revenue are not profit. Acquisition spend, campaign attribution, full margins and service costs are unavailable.
- There are no committed predictive models, performance measurements, dashboard screenshots or intervention results.
